This guide walks you through connecting your **TallyPrime** company with your software application. It takes less than 2 minutes and requires no technical expertise.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Click "Connect Tally" in Your Software
Log into your web software (billing, CRM, or financing platform) and click **"Connect Tally"**.

### Step 2: Note Your Activation Code
A 6-character code will appear on your screen (for example: `TC-7837`). Keep this tab open.

### Step 3: Download Tally Connect
Click the **"Download Tally Connect for Windows"** button to download `TallyConnectAgentSetup.exe`.

### Step 4: Open TallyPrime on Your Computer
Ensure TallyPrime is open with the company you want to link.

### Step 5: Run the Setup File
Double-click `TallyConnectAgentSetup.exe`. You will see a clean setup screen:

```text
===============================================================
📦 Tally Connect Setup
===============================================================
Welcome! This setup will link your TallyPrime in a few moments.

Enter your 6-character Activation Code: TC-7837

[3/5] Verifying activation code...                 ✔ Code Verified

[5/5] Setting up automatic background sync...      ✔ Enabled (starts with Windows)

===============================================================
🎉 Connected Successfully!
Tally Connect is now linked to your company.
Everything is set up and running quietly in the background.
You can now return to your browser.

```

### Step 6: Choose What Data to Share
Back in your web browser, select which data categories you allow your software to access:
- [x] **Customers** (Customer names, GSTINs, and addresses)
- [x] **Vendors** (Supplier names and balances)
- [x] **Invoices / Sales** (Sales register vouchers)
- [ ] **Inventory** (Stock item balances — *optional*)

### Step 7: Click "Sync Now"
Click **"Sync Now"** in your web software. Your records will synchronize in seconds.

## Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### Does Tally Connect change my accounting data?
**No.** Tally Connect is read-only. It never modifies, alters, or creates transactions in your TallyPrime books unless you explicitly use an authorized invoicing feature.

### Does Tally Connect start automatically when I restart my PC?
**Yes.** Tally Connect runs quietly in the background on Windows startup, so you never have to re-enter your activation code.

### What if Tally is closed when the computer turns on?
Tally Connect waits quietly. As soon as you open TallyPrime, connection resumes automatically.

### What if I see "Tally Not Running"?
1. Verify TallyPrime is running on your computer.
2. Ensure your company is loaded in TallyPrime.
3. If still offline, right-click `TallyConnectAgent.exe` and select **Run as Administrator**.
